About us

Every thread has a name behind it

WovenHaat was born from a simple belief: when something is made by hand, its story deserves to reach your home.

Our story

From Panipat's looms to your living room

In 2019, we started with a small weaving unit in Panipat — three pit looms, one dye house, and an uncompromising commitment: never compromise on quality, and always pay fair compensation to artisans.

Today, our network spans 48 artisan families across Bhuj, Bhadohi, and Kannur, crafting everything from rugs to curtains on traditional handlooms. Every order carries their name and their handcraft.

Milestones

Our Journey

  1. 2019

    The First Loom

    First collection launched in Panipat with three pit looms — 6 designs, 100 orders.

  2. 2021

    GOTS Certification

    Organic cotton line certified — every fibre is now traceable back to the farm.

  3. 2023

    48 Families & 62% Less Water

    Bhujodi and Kannur clusters joined; our dye house reduced water consumption by 62% through rainwater harvesting.

  4. 2025

    18 Countries, One Loom List

    Alongside exports, our D2C store went live — now serving buyers across 18+ countries, delivered straight to your home.

Craft process

From Fibre to Finishing — Four Steps

01

Fibre

GOTS-certified organic cotton and GRS recycled yarn — ethically sourced from audited suppliers.

02

Dyeing

Low-impact and natural dyes, ETP-treated water, saving 62% water per kg of yarn.

03

Weaving

Woven by hand on pit looms — each piece takes 2–5 days, depending on design complexity.

04

Finishing

Washing, blocking, and rigorous quality checks — textile scraps are recycled, achieving zero landfill waste.

What we stand for

Three Promises We Never Break

Handmade, always

Zero power looms. Every rug, cushion, and throw is crafted by artisan hands — subtle irregularities are the hallmark of authentic craft, not flaws.

Fair work

Wages well above minimum wage, well-ventilated spaces, regulated hours, and annual bonuses. 60% of our weaving units are women-led.

Sustainable materials

Organic cotton, recycled fibres, natural dyes, and 100% plastic-free packaging — committed to zero-landfill operations.
